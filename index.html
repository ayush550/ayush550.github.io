About me

I hold a master's degree in Information Security from IIT Jammu my master's thesis is based on "blockchain e-voting architecture using Decentralised Identifiers", the main goal of this thesis is to ensure every vote is verifiable and authentic.

I have around three years of industry experience across Samsung R&D, Nvidia and the Agriculture Insurance Company of India, and I enjoy turning theory into working systems.

Background

I grew up in a rural area and learned mostly by rote in my native language. My parents moved me to a convent school, where I found a love for science and mathematics and built a solar-powered water purifier for a science competition. Participated in Environmental walkathons, Debates and Art.

During my B.Tech I built a speed-monitoring app for school vans and began teaching underprivileged children in my neighbourhood. I cleared GATE and joined IIT Jammu, where I worked as a teaching assistant for Algorithms and Database Management Systems, handling classes of over 100 students.

In an adversarial machine learning course, my team fooled a pre-trained U-Net with perturbed inputs. It showed me the limits of modern AI and why trustworthy models matter.

I believe education can change circumstances. Teaching is something I enjoy and am good at, and research satisfies my curiosity. A PhD lets me refine my research skills and prepare for a long-term academic career.
